# 🎬 Netflix Movies & TV Shows — EDA
Exploratory data analysis on the Netflix titles dataset, covering data cleaning, feature engineering, and univariate, bivariate, and multivariate analysis.

---

## 📊 Dataset
Netflix Movies and TV Shows (Kaggle) — 8,807 titles with type, director, cast, country, release year, rating, duration, and genre.

---

## 🧰 Tools
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn

---

## 🔍 What's Inside?
Missing value treatment (director, cast, country, duration) and duplicate checks
Feature engineering: year_added, content_age, main_genre, primary_country, duration_num, genre_count
Univariate, bivariate, and multivariate analysis with visualizations

---

## 🔑 Key Insights
Movies outnumber TV Shows across most genres
TV-MA and TV-14 are the most common ratings
The US leads content production, followed by India and the UK
Catalog growth accelerated sharply after 2016
Most titles are added to Netflix within a year or two of release
